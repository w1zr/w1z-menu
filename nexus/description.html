w1z's menu

New in 1.3.0: Weapon Upgrades — add one specific upgrade to any gun, like a scope on the hunting rifle or a bigger magazine on the 9mm.

Since 1.2.0: a playable Ellie that really works — Ellie moves, aims, shoots and crafts in every chapter, Joel's 9mm grip is correct, and Joel follows her as the AI companion.

An in-game mod menu for The Last of Us Part I. Press Insert or F9 (controller: L3 + R3) and change the player, weapons, inventory, outfits, camera and more while you play. You don't need to finish the game first.

Almost everything in the menu is Naughty Dog's own code: the game still contains the studio's developer menu, and w1z's menu uses its flags and functions directly instead of poking random memory. Anything the menu can't find in your version of the game is greyed out with the reason — everything else keeps working.

Features

Player

  • God Mode, No Hit Reactions, Infinite Sprint, Undetectable Player, One Hit Kills

Character — Playable Ellie that really works (since 1.2.0)

  • Play as Ellie in Joel's chapters and it actually works: she walks, runs, climbs, aims, shoots and reloads with her own animations.
  • Crafting works — Tab opens the crafting menu as Ellie in every chapter, not just Winter.
  • Pistol grip works — Joel's 9mm sits straight in her hands with proper recoil.
  • Replace Campaign Ellie with Joel (on by default): the story companion becomes Joel — the same companion with her AI and scripted moments, in Joel's body.
  • Pick Ellie's outfit or let it match the chapter. Swap Now switches right away; Play as Joel in Winter is also included.

Weapons and Items

  • Infinite Ammo with normal reloads (F7) and No Reload (F8, player only) — the HUD shows ∞
  • Infinite Weapon Health, Disable Weapon Sway, Disable Auto Reload, Disable Auto Holster, Drop All Weapons
  • Weapon Upgrades (new in 1.3.0): pick one upgrade for a gun — scope, magazine capacity, fire rate, reload speed, damage, recoil, range and more for the 9mm, Revolver, El Diablo, Shorty, Shotgun, Hunting Rifle, Semi-Auto Rifle, both bows, Flamethrower and holsters; Remove All Upgrades per gun
  • Give Joel (9 guns), Give Ellie (3), Give Melee (6), Give Items (shivs, health kits, molotovs, bombs, bricks, bottles, crafting materials)
  • Infinite Crafting, Infinite Craftables

Inventory

  • Give All + Upgrades, Give Each Craftable, Weapon Parts x10 / x100, Pills x10 / x100, All Recipes / No Recipes

Outfits and Unlocks

  • Change your outfit in-game from Pause > Skins (and Extras > Skins) without finishing the story — every skin owned
  • Give / Remove Treasures, Give Journal Pages, Reset Journal
  • All Chapters, All Encounters, All Extras — only while the menu is loaded, never written to your profile

Camera

  • Manual Camera (free camera), Teleport Player To Camera, Hide HUD

Scenes and Misc

  • Replay Current Scene, Game Speed (experimental)

Menu

  • Keyboard, mouse and controller; Search (just start typing) and Favorites (Tab / X)
  • The game gets no input while the menu is open
  • Settings save automatically to Documents\w1z's menu\w1zsMenu.ini, so updates never reset them; Feature Status page shows what loaded

Controls

  • Open / close: Insert or F9 — controller L3 + R3
  • Move: Up / Down or mouse — D-pad
  • Select: Enter or click — A / Cross
  • Back: Esc, Backspace or right click — B / Circle
  • Favorite: Tab — X / Square

Installation

Fluffy Mod Manager (recommended)

  1. Put w1zsMenu-v1.3.0.zip in Fluffy Mod Manager\Games\TLOU\Mods\.
  2. Open Fluffy and tick w1z's menu. Untick it to uninstall.

Manual

  1. Copy winmm.dll from the zip into the folder that contains tlou-i.exe
    (e.g. C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\The Last of Us Part I\).
  2. To uninstall, delete winmm.dll, w1zsMenu.log and w1zsMenu-README.txt from that folder.

Install while the game is closed. No other loader is needed. modinfo.ini and screenshot.png in the zip are only for Fluffy.

Compatibility

  • Tested: Steam, build 18995818 (game version 1.1.5).
  • Not tested: Epic Games Store.
  • Loads as winmm.dll, so it works alongside version.dll and xinput1_4.dll mods.
  • Includes everything from my TLOU Infinite Ammo and TLOU Outfit Changer mods — you only need the menu. If either is still installed, the menu leaves that part to it.
  • Compatible with outfit/texture mods installed through Fluffy.
  • If a game update changes code the menu relies on, that feature is greyed out with the reason in the menu and in w1zsMenu.log.

Saves

The menu never writes save files itself. Giving items, weapons, parts, treasures or journal pages changes your current game, and the game saves that at its next checkpoint like any other change.

Known issues

  • Playing as Ellie: her arm clips through the bow when it's put away, and the shotgun and hunting rifle twitch in from the right when aiming. The companion Joel speaks Ellie's lines in her voice.
  • In Encounters, a weapon's magazine size can change after a restart. This is the game's own behaviour — it also happens with no mods installed.
  • Pause While Open is not available yet (the game still gets no input while the menu is open).
  • Scene Teleport only offers Replay Current Scene for now.
